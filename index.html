The Catherine Royale profile

A table shaped
by the seasons.

At Catherine Royale, every detail begins with the season. We work closely with the growers, makers and fishermen just beyond our doorstep, letting exceptional ingredients tell their own stories.

The result is a style of French dining that feels familiar yet surprising: generous, quietly inventive and best shared around the table. Catherine Royale is an invitation to slow down, savour each course and enjoy the company around you.

From seasonal menus and a considered beverage bar to VIP dining and private lounge experiences, every part of the evening is designed to feel personal and welcoming.
